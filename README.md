## versions
There are two available versions (games)
1. Catch the cookie
   It was the initial idea to make cathing cookie game, however later I decided to completely change my game
   link to game 1 catch the cookie [https://sergecage.github.io/Gizmo/main/]

2. Run, catch and collect cookie avoid enemies
   This game is more interactive, the task is collect cookies and avoid enemies.
   Link to the game 2 [https://sergecage.github.io/Gizmo/Gizmo2/Gizmo2.0/]

   Available level 1 (the easiest level with the task of collecting 10 cookies and avoiding enemies).
   And hardcore mode available on play button (the task is to survive for 100 seconds, collect cookies and avoid enemies)
